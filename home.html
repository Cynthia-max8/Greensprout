About Green Sprout

Green Sprout Learning Centre is a nurturing early childhood institution located in Kamuthi area, past Maziwa police. We offer quality education in Daycare, Playgroup, PP1, and PP2 through the CBC curriculum. Our dedicated teachers create a safe, fun, and creative learning environment where every child is encouraged to grow and shine.
